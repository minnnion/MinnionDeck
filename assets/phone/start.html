One-time setup

So the connection to your PC is private and your phone never warns about it, install your PC's certificate on this phone once.

1. Download the certificate
  1. Tap Download above, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded, then Install.
  3. Open Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn on MinnionDeck on this PC.
  4. Come back here and tap Open my deck.
