# Privacy, in short

The full policy is at **[minniondeck.github.io/privacy](https://minniondeck.github.io/privacy/)**. The short version:

- **No account, no ads, no tracking.** MinnionDeck sends us no usage data.
- **Your data stays on your PC.** Profiles, buttons, pictures and settings live in the folder you choose, encrypted with a key made for your install.
- **The app only contacts GitHub** to check for updates and to download Marketplace plugins.
- **Phones connect over your own Wi-Fi**, and the picture editor runs on your own PC (127.0.0.1). Nothing goes through our servers.
- **Plugins talk to their own services directly.** Discord can control your voice, camera and screen share once you press Authorize; its login is kept encrypted on your PC. Spotify and Weather (Open-Meteo) work the same way. We never see that data.
- **Logs stay on your PC** unless you attach one to a bug report.
- **Not for children under 13.**
