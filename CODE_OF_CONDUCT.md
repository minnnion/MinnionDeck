# Code of conduct

MinnionDeck's issues, Discussions and plugin submissions are for everyone who uses or builds for it.

- **Be kind and patient.** People here range from first-time streamers to seasoned developers.
- **Stay on topic** and keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- **No harassment**, insults, hate, sexual content, or sharing anyone's private information.
- **No spam**, ads or links to pirated software.

Posts that break these rules are edited or removed, and repeated or serious cases are blocked from
the repository. To report something, email **minniondeck@gmail.com** or use GitHub's
**Report content** on the post.
